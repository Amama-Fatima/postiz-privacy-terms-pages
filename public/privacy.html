Privacy Policy

How we protect your social media workspace.

This Privacy Policy explains how King of Automation collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our social media scheduling and automation service, including when you connect Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X/Twitter, LinkedIn, YouTube, Reddit, or other social accounts supported in the service.

1. Scope

King of Automation LLC, doing business as King of Automation, ("King of Automation," "we," "us," or "our") provides a self-service platform for planning, scheduling, publishing, and managing social media content. This Policy applies to our website, hosted application, support communications, and related services.

This Policy does not replace the privacy policies of the social media platforms you connect. Those platforms process information under their own policies and developer terms.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information needed to operate the service, including:

  • Account information: name, email address, password credentials, profile details, business name, and billing or plan information when applicable.
  • Connected account information: social account IDs, page IDs, usernames, profile names, profile images, access tokens, refresh tokens, permissions granted, and connection status.
  • Content and media: posts, captions, hashtags, images, videos, thumbnails, drafts, scheduling times, approval notes, and publishing history you create or upload.
  • Usage and device information: log data, IP address, browser type, device identifiers, pages viewed, feature usage, error reports, and approximate location derived from technical data.
  • Support communications: messages, attachments, and other information you send when requesting help.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • create, secure, and administer your account;
  • connect your selected social media accounts through OAuth or platform APIs;
  • schedule, publish, retrieve, and manage content at your direction;
  • display connected profiles, pages, channels, calendars, post status, and analytics where available;
  • send service notices, security alerts, support replies, and product updates;
  • monitor reliability, troubleshoot errors, prevent abuse, and protect the service;
  • comply with legal obligations and platform developer requirements.

4. Connected Platform Data

When you connect a third-party platform such as Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X/Twitter, LinkedIn, YouTube, or Reddit, you authorize us to access only the information and permissions you approve during the connection flow.

We use platform access tokens to perform actions you request, such as listing available pages, showing profile information, publishing scheduled posts, retrieving publish status, importing media metadata, or showing analytics if that feature is available.

For YouTube and other Google API integrations, our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your use of YouTube features is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke access to Google data from your Google account permissions page at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

We do not sell connected platform data. We do not use social platform data for advertising profiles. We do not post to your social accounts unless you or an authorized user of your workspace schedules, publishes, or approves that action.

5. How We Share Information

We share information only where needed to provide or protect the service:

  • Social media platforms: we send content, media, captions, and related data to the platforms you choose so they can publish or process your requested actions.
  • Service providers: we may use hosting, storage, analytics, email, payment, security, and support vendors that process information on our behalf.
  • Workspace members: information may be visible to users you invite to your workspace according to their role.
  • Legal and safety reasons: we may disclose information if required by law, to enforce our terms, or to protect rights, safety, and service integrity.
  • Business transfers: information may be transferred if we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain account, workspace, connected account, and content data for as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security, and enforce our agreements.

You can disconnect a social account from your King of Automation workspace at any time. Disconnecting removes our ability to use the related access token for future actions, but it does not delete content already published to a third-party platform.

To request account deletion or removal of personal information, email contact@kingofautomation.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request." We may need to verify your identity and authority over the account before completing the request.

7. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information, connected account credentials, and content data. These safeguards may include access controls, encrypted transport, restricted credential handling, monitoring, and least-privilege operational practices.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your login credentials secure, maintaining access to your email account, and promptly notifying us if you suspect unauthorized use.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • access, correct, export, or delete personal information;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • disconnect social accounts or revoke platform permissions;
  • opt out of non-essential marketing communications.

You can also revoke King of Automation's access from the settings area of the relevant third-party platform. Some requests may be limited by legal, security, anti-fraud, or operational retention requirements.

9. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, measure service performance, troubleshoot errors, and protect against abuse. You can control cookies through your browser settings, but disabling some cookies may affect service functionality.

10. International Use

Your information may be processed in countries other than where you live. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for international transfers and take steps designed to protect information according to this Policy.

11. Children

The service is intended for business and professional use. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes may also be communicated through the service or by email.
